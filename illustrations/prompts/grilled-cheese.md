# Grilled Cheese — illustration prompts

Base hero color is muted beige/brown `#A39068` (toasted bread), joined by stronger food yellow
`#F0D474` (melted cheese) wherever cheese is the visible focus.

## Hero (finished dish)

**SUBJECT:** A grilled cheese sandwich cut in half on a simple plate, melted cheese visible at the
cut edge, viewed from a slightly elevated three-quarter angle. Show only the sandwich halves and
plate.

**HERO COLOR(S):** stronger food yellow `#F0D474`, muted beige/brown accent `#A39068`

**SUPPORTING NEUTRAL:** none
