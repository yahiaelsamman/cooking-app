# Avocado Toast — illustration prompts

Base hero color is muted herb green `#8A9A5B` (avocado), joined by stronger food yellow `#F0D474`
(egg yolk) in the steps where the egg is the focus, matching the guide's 1–2 accent color limit.

## Hero (finished dish)

**SUBJECT:** A slice of toasted bread topped with a mound of mashed avocado and a fried egg,
